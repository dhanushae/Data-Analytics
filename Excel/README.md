# E-Commerce Delivery Analytics Dashboard (Excel)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project is an **Excel dashboard** designed to analyze **E-Commerce delivery performance** across multiple quick-commerce platforms such as **Blinkit, Instamart, and JioMart**.  
The dashboard provides actionable insights into **orders, revenue, delivery efficiency, service quality, and refunds**, enabling stakeholders to track operational performance and customer experience at a glance.
The goal of this project is to demonstrate **data analysis, visualization, and business storytelling skills using Microsoft Excel**.

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives
- Analyze overall **order volume and revenue trends**
- Monitor **delivery efficiency and on-time performance**
- Understand **service rating distribution**
- Identify **platform-wise and category-wise performance**
- Provide an **interactive and user-friendly reporting experience**

---

## 📊 KPIs Tracked
| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| **Total Orders** | Total number of orders processed |
| **Total Revenue** | Total revenue generated |
| **Average Delivery Time** | Mean delivery time across all orders |
| **Average Service Rating** | Customer satisfaction score |
| **On-Time Delivery %** | Percentage of orders delivered on time |
| **Refund Requested %** | Percentage of orders with refund requests |

## 📊 Dashboard Charts Explanation

### 1️⃣ Sales by Category
- **Chart Type:** Column Chart  
- **Purpose:** Compares sales across major product categories such as Bakery, Beverages, Snacks, etc.  
- **Insight:** Helps identify top revenue-generating categories and understand customer purchasing preferences.

---

### 2️⃣ Orders by Platform
- **Chart Type:** Column Chart  
- **Purpose:** Displays the total number of orders across platforms like Blinkit, Instamart, and JioMart.  
- **Insight:** Highlights the platform contributing the highest order volume.

---

### 3️⃣ Revenue by Product Category
- **Chart Type:** Horizontal Bar Chart  
- **Purpose:** Shows revenue contribution by different product categories across platforms.  
- **Insight:** Identifies which categories drive the most revenue and deserve strategic focus.

---

### 4️⃣ Delivery Status Breakdown
- **Chart Type:** Donut Chart  
- **Purpose:** Visualizes the proportion of on-time deliveries versus delayed deliveries.  
- **Insight:** Provides a quick view of overall delivery efficiency and logistics performance.

---

### 5️⃣ Monthly Average Delivery Time Trend
- **Chart Type:** Line Chart  
- **Purpose:** Tracks average delivery time month-by-month throughout the year.  
- **Insight:** Helps detect seasonal trends, delays, and improvements in delivery operations.

---

### 6️⃣ Service Rating Distribution
- **Chart Type:** Column Chart  
- **Purpose:** Shows the distribution of customer service ratings from 1 to 5.  
- **Insight:** Evaluates overall customer satisfaction and highlights areas for service improvement.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters (Slicers)
The dashboard includes slicers for:
- **Platform** (Blinkit, Instamart, JioMart)
- **Service Rating**
- **Order Month**
- **Delivery Delay (Yes/No)**

These slicers allow users to dynamically explore data and focus on specific segments.
