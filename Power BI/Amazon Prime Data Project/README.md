## 📌 Project Overview
This project is a **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze **Amazon Prime Video content data**, focusing on **Movies and TV Shows** available on the platform.  
The dashboard provides actionable insights into **content volume, ratings, genres, countries, and release trends**, helping stakeholders understand content distribution and strategy at a glance.

The goal of this project is to demonstrate **Power BI data modeling, DAX calculations, visualization skills, and business storytelling**.

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives
- Analyze overall **content volume** on Prime Video
- Understand **Movies vs TV Shows distribution**
- Identify **top genres and content ratings**
- Analyze **country-wise content performance**
- Track **content release trends over time**
- Provide an **interactive and stakeholder-friendly analytics dashboard**

---

## 📊 KPIs Tracked
| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| **Total Titles** | Total number of titles available on Prime Video |
| **Total Movies** | Total number of movie titles |
| **Total TV Shows** | Total number of TV show titles |
| **Movies Proportion** | Percentage of movies in total content |
| **Average Duration** | Average runtime of content (in minutes) |

---

## 📊 Dashboard Charts Explanation

### 1️⃣ Top Titles by Rating
- **Chart Type:** Column Chart  
- **Purpose:** Displays the count of titles by content ratings such as R, PG-13, TV-14, TV-PG, etc.  
- **Insight:** Helps understand Prime Video’s audience targeting and content maturity distribution.

---

### 2️⃣ Movies vs TV Shows
- **Chart Type:** Donut Chart  
- **Purpose:** Compares the proportion of Movies and TV Shows available on the platform.  
- **Insight:** Reveals Prime Video’s stronger focus on movie-based content.

---

### 3️⃣ Top Titles by Genre
- **Chart Type:** Horizontal Bar Chart  
- **Purpose:** Shows the number of titles across genres such as International, Anime, Unscripted, Sports, Fantasy, etc.  
- **Insight:** Identifies dominant genres and highlights Prime Video’s emphasis on diverse and global content.

---

### 4️⃣ Top Rated Countries
- **Chart Type:** Horizontal Bar Chart  
- **Purpose:** Displays countries contributing the highest number of top-rated titles.  
- **Insight:** Highlights strong content performance in regions like the United States, India, the United Kingdom, and Canada.

---

### 5️⃣ Content Count by Year, Quarter, and Month
- **Chart Type:** Line Chart  
- **Purpose:** Tracks the number of titles added over time.  
- **Insight:** Helps identify content release patterns, growth phases, and strategic release periods.
