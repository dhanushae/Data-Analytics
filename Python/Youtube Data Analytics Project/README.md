# Trending YouTube Videos Data Analysis (Python)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes **Trending YouTube Videos** using Python to uncover patterns in **content popularity, engagement, categories, and publishing trends**.  
By leveraging data analysis and visualization techniques, the project aims to understand what factors contribute to videos trending on YouTube.

The analysis focuses on **views, likes, comments, categories, publish timing, and engagement metrics**, helping derive actionable insights for content creators, marketers, and analysts.

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives
- Analyze trending video performance based on **views, likes, and comments**
- Identify **top-performing categories** on YouTube
- Understand **engagement patterns** across different content types
- Analyze **publishing trends** (day, time, frequency)
- Explore relationships between **views, likes, and comments**
- Provide **data-driven insights** into YouTube trending behavior

---

## 🗂 Dataset Description
- **Source:** YouTube Trending Videos dataset  
- **Key Columns:**
  - `video_id`
  - `title`
  - `channel_title`
  - `category`
  - `publish_time`
  - `views`
  - `likes`
  - `dislikes`
  - `comment_count`
  - `trending_date`

---

## 📈 Charts & Visualizations Explanation

### 1️⃣ Top 10 Trending Video Categories
- **Chart Type:** Bar Chart  
- **Purpose:** Identify which content categories appear most frequently in trending videos  
- **Insight:** Entertainment and Music dominate trending content, indicating higher audience demand and engagement  

---

### 2️⃣ Views Distribution of Trending Videos
- **Chart Type:** Histogram  
- **Purpose:** Understand how views are distributed among trending videos  
- **Insight:** Majority of trending videos fall within a specific view range, with a few viral outliers  

---

### 3️⃣ Likes vs Views Relationship
- **Chart Type:** Scatter Plot  
- **Purpose:** Analyze correlation between video views and likes  
- **Insight:** Strong positive correlation indicates higher views generally result in higher likes  

---

### 4️⃣ Comments vs Views Analysis
- **Chart Type:** Scatter Plot  
- **Purpose:** Measure audience interaction relative to video popularity  
- **Insight:** Videos with higher views tend to receive more comments, reflecting audience engagement  

---

### 5️⃣ Category-wise Average Engagement
- **Chart Type:** Bar Chart  
- **Purpose:** Compare average likes and comments across categories  
- **Insight:** Music and Gaming show higher engagement per video compared to other categories  

---

### 6️⃣ Trending Videos by Publish Day
- **Chart Type:** Bar Chart  
- **Purpose:** Identify which days videos are more likely to trend  
- **Insight:** Videos published mid-week and weekends show higher trending frequency  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Entertainment and Music categories dominate YouTube trending videos  
- Engagement metrics (likes and comments) strongly correlate with views  
- Viral videos significantly skew overall view distribution  
- Publishing time and day influence a video’s trending potential  
- High engagement rate is a strong indicator of trending success
